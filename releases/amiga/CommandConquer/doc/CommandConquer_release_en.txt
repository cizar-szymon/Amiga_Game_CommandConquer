COMMAND & CONQUER: TIBERIAN DAWN for the Amiga - history of releases
====================================================================

How to report a bug: give the release number (Shell: Version CommandConquer),
your Amiga and card, and what you were doing. Files RAM:cnc-log.txt and
RAM:cnc-sound-log.txt are helpful.

0.1  (23.11.2026)  first release
  - Tiberian Dawn, GDI and Nod campaigns, 640x400, 256 colours, mouse control.
  - Sound and music through AHI (default on a Warp card) or the Amiga's own
    audio chip (Paula); Tool Types SOUND=AHI / PAULA / OFF.
  - Start from the icon or from a Shell; Ctrl-C closes the game.
  - Game movies are skipped.
