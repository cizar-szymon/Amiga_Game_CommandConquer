COMMAND & CONQUER: TIBERIAN DAWN for the Amiga (Warp cards)         release 0.1
==========================================================================

Port of the GPL engine "Vanilla Conquer" (the open-source Tiberian Dawn
by Electronic Arts) to AmigaOS 3.x with its own native back end.

HARDWARE
  Made for Amiga 500 / 1200 / 3000 / 4000 with a Warp card (Warp 560, 1240,
  1260, 1260 Extreme, 7060), ported and tested on such cards: it reaches
  its speed through the fast graphics (RTG) of the Warp. Other Amigas with a
  68040/68060 and Picasso96 RTG run it, but slower.
  Tested on: A1200 + Warp 1260 Extreme (68060, 100 MHz), AmigaOS 3.2.2.1.

REQUIREMENTS
  AmigaOS 3.2, Picasso96, 68060.library (or 040), AHI for sound (on a Warp
  card AHI plays through HDMI), the game data (see data/ReadMe.txt).

INSTALLATION
  UnZip CommandConquer-0.1.zip -d Work:Games
  Put the *.MIX files of Command & Conquer (Gold) into CommandConquer/data
  (the freeware data from Electronic Arts, NOT included).
  Start CommandConquer from its icon or from a Shell.

SOUND (Tool Types of the icon, remove the brackets to switch on)
  SOUND=AHI    sound through AHI (default on a Warp card)
  SOUND=PAULA  sound through the Amiga's own audio chip
  SOUND=OFF    no sound

THE GAME
  640x400 screen with 256 colours, played with the mouse as on the PC.
  The sidebar is opened by the "Sidebar" tab at the top right.
  Saved games and settings: the drawer "user" in the game's drawer.
  The game movies are skipped for now.
  Ctrl-C closes the game.

BUG REPORTS
  Please state the release number (Shell: Version CommandConquer) and the
  Amiga/card you use. Files RAM:cnc-log.txt and RAM:cnc-sound-log.txt help.
